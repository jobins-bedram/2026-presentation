I still remember my first assignments, it was at the CRM project, And the thing I remember most isn't the code — it's meeting Puncoz-san. Watching him work sparked a dream in me: I wanted to become an excellent engineer as him.

I spent my early days on building the CRM system, learning the codebase, the team, the domain. I built features like clients’ import. It was classic software engineering — requirements in, code out, ship, repeat. And I enjoyed it.

In 2022 I moved from the CRM onto the JoBins core product — the main platform[jobins.jp] the business runs on. The work didn't change much: the same engineering, bigger stage. With the team we shipped job management, candidate management, and more. I was comfortable — and that's important for what comes next.

By early 2023, But one thing kept nagging at me was resume parsing. It worked — but it was expensive, and it wasn't ours. and I hold that thought, because that little itch changed the direction of my career.
